03 — THE FIVE PILLARS

How modern businesses are delivering Relevize's service — and growing from it.

Each pillar below upgrades a piece of what Relevize already does. Together they compose a platform that grows existing accounts, unifies departments, and commands a 5x valuation multiple in 18 months.

01 Data Enrichment

Ecosystem-Led Growth (ELG)

Turn the partner ecosystem into the primary signal source for expansion.
What it is

Crossbeam + Reveal (now merged, 25,000+ companies) automate partner account overlap in real time so co-marketing runs against shared opportunity signals — not static briefs.

Growth Lever

Intercom: 30% YoY partner-sourced, 157% partner-influenced revenue. Crossbeam cohort: $86B in open expansion on existing accounts.

Dept Unlock

Copilot sidebars inside Salesforce + HubSpot; Slack #no-partner-attached alerts fire on Solution Review opps; partner managers embedded in weekly forecast calls.

Ref: Crossbeam · Reveal · Copilot for Salesforce/HubSpot

02 Infrastructure

Composable CDP

Let the client's warehouse be the source of truth; activate audiences out, not in.
What it is

Hightouch / Census / GrowthLoop pattern — entity resolution, audience modeling, and reverse-ETL activation all on top of Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks.

Growth Lever

Ramp drove 25% of total pipeline from warehouse-native prospecting. One audience definition powers LinkedIn, CRM, CS tool, and Slack simultaneously.

Dept Unlock

Single shared schema across marketing, sales, CS, and finance — kills the "why does your dashboard differ from mine" meeting forever.

Ref: Hightouch · Census · GrowthLoop · Snowflake · Databricks

03 Collaboration

Data Clean Rooms

Replace the partner marketing brief with a privacy-safe data collaboration.
What it is

Snowflake / AWS / LiveRamp (+ Habu) clean rooms let Workday and a partner combine 1P data to find overlap without exposing PII. The brief becomes a live collaboration.

Growth Lever

Each new partner = a fresh addressable overlap audience from the same customer book. Workday's ~80 campaigns/year become 80 unique expansion audiences.

Dept Unlock

Legal + privacy + IT approve once. Marketing, analytics, partnerships operate inside the same governance layer.

Ref: Snowflake Clean Rooms · AWS Clean Rooms · LiveRamp · Decentriq

04 Measurement

LinkedIn CAPI

Trade form-fill vanity metrics for pipeline-stage optimization.
What it is

Server-to-server pipe feeds CRM pipeline stages (SQL, Opportunity, Closed-Won) back into LinkedIn so the algorithm optimizes toward revenue. Recovers ~40% of lost data.

Growth Lever

~20% CPA reduction benchmark (Dreamdata). QBRs report pipeline-attributed ROI — the single best churn insurance on the existing book.

Dept Unlock

Forces one shared definition of "conversion" across marketing, sales, RevOps, and finance. One schema, one truth.

Ref: LinkedIn CAPI · Dreamdata · HockeyStack · Factors · LiveRamp

05 Automation

Agentic AI

Three autonomous agents that 10x throughput on flat headcount.
What it is

(a) Partner-onboarding agent replaces the brief, (b) campaign-ops agent runs LinkedIn budget/creative/pacing daily, (c) MDF agent reallocates budget proactively. Coordinated by a context layer.

Growth Lever

Xerox scaled 11 → 100 premier partners using AI-automated paid media. Each agent becomes a separately priceable SKU on every existing contract.

Dept Unlock

Agents operate on the context graph, not on departmental artifacts. No more work being "moved between tools" by humans.

Ref: Salesforce Agentforce · Snowflake · Impartner · Allbound · TreasureData
